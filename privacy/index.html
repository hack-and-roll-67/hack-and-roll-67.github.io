Privacy Policy

Effective 28 October 2026

This policy explains what data Reel Studio ("the app", "we") handles when a TikTok or Instagram account is connected to it, why, and what you can do about it.

1. Who is responsible

Reel Studio is a personal tool operated by Hack and Roll, who is responsible for the data described here. Contact: hack.and.roll.67@gmail.com.

Reel Studio is not offered to the public. Only accounts that belong to the operator are connected to it.

2. What data we handle

When an account is connected through TikTok Login Kit or Instagram Login, we receive and store:

  • Account identifiers and profile basics: the account's ID (TikTok open_id or Instagram user ID), username or display name, and profile picture URL.
  • Access tokens: the OAuth access token, refresh token and their expiry dates, which let the app post on the account's behalf.
  • Posting settings from TikTok: before each post we fetch the creator information TikTok provides (nickname, available privacy options, interaction settings and posting limits) so the right choices can be shown. These are used for that post only.
  • Records of our own posts: the caption, scheduled time, status, and the post or publish ID and link returned by the platform.

We do not collect followers, messages, comments, contacts, analytics, location, payment details or any content created by other people.

3. How we use it

  • To upload and publish videos the operator has created or approved, to the account they were scheduled for.
  • To show which account a video will be posted to and which privacy options are available.
  • To keep the connection working (refreshing tokens) and to show whether a post succeeded, with a link to it.

We do not use this data for advertising or profiling, do not sell or rent it, and do not use it to train AI models.

4. Where it is stored

All of the data above is kept in a local database on the operator's own computer. The app's dashboard only answers requests from that computer, or from a browser logged in with the operator's password. Tokens are only ever sent to the platform they belong to.

5. Who receives data

  • TikTok and Meta (Instagram) receive the video, its caption and the chosen settings when we publish. Their handling is governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy and the Instagram Privacy Policy.
  • Temporary video hosting: when a platform downloads a video from a link rather than receiving it directly, the video file is made available through an expiring, signed link (served through a Cloudflare tunnel) or through cloud storage from which it is deleted after publishing. Only the video file is shared this way.
  • AI and voice services (such as Anthropic and ElevenLabs) are used to write and narrate videos. They receive only the text needed for that, never data from a connected TikTok or Instagram account.

We share data with nobody else, except where the law requires it.

6. How long we keep it

Tokens and profile data are kept while the account stays connected. Disconnecting an account in the app deletes its tokens, profile data and post records. Videos that were published stay on TikTok or Instagram until you delete them there.

7. Your choices and rights

  • You can revoke Reel Studio's access at any time in your TikTok or Instagram settings, in the list of apps and services connected to your account. Revoking access stops all posting.
  • You can ask us to show, correct or delete the data we hold about you, or object to its use, by writing to hack.and.roll.67@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.
  • If you are in the EU or EEA, you also have the rights given by the GDPR, including the right to complain to your data protection authority.

8. Security

Data stays on the operator's computer, the dashboard only accepts requests from the operator, and links to video files are signed and expire. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect the data.

9. Children

Reel Studio is not intended for, and does not knowingly handle data of, anyone under 18.

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log visitors' IP addresses for security; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.
