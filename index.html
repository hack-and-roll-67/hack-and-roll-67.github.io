Reel Studio

A personal tool that creates short vertical videos and publishes them to its operator's own TikTok and Instagram accounts.

Reel Studio runs on its operator's computer. It renders videos (piano covers, quote cards, short stories and data maps), lets the operator review and edit each video and its caption, and posts it on a schedule the operator sets. It is not a public service: only accounts that belong to the operator are connected to it.

CreateVideos are rendered from templates; captions are written or reviewed by the operator.
ReviewEach video can be previewed, edited and approved before it is posted.
PublishPosts go out through the official TikTok and Instagram APIs, with the visibility the operator chooses.

Connected platforms

  • TikTok, through TikTok Login Kit and the Content Posting API.
  • Instagram, through the Instagram API with Instagram Login.

Reel Studio only posts videos the operator has created or approved, only to the account they connected, and never reads followers, messages or other people's content.
